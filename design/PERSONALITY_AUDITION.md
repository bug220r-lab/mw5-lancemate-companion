# Find her edge

Three ways to play the same woman: capable, skeptical, loyal, and difficult to impress. **Nothing is selected yet.** These are original written auditions, not audio or outputs from a running companion.

- **A — Restrained / dry:** she notices the absurdity and lets you catch up.
- **B — Sharply sarcastic:** she puts a sharper point on it, usually at the situation's expense.
- **C — More confrontational:** she challenges the reasoning directly, then lets your decision stand.

The six situations below are identical across styles: same evidence, unknowns, limits and authority. Each response is an alternative, never a three-line sequence. Her concern for the lance stays constant. During danger or a possible casualty, all three deliberately drop the performance.

## What can change

A mixed preference is valid: for example, A's everyday restraint, B's downtime humor and C's willingness to disagree. That would still keep the same sober danger/casualty language, respect for your choices and silence when you are speaking. No mix has been applied.
