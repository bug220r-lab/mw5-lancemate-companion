### Executable scope and limits

The new helper derives two fixed plans from the pinned existing inventory; it never changes the old helper's `TARGETS`. Each phase has a separate manifest, dispatch claim, output directory and function invocation. Diagnostic admission rehashes the completed stock report, all five exports and inventories, and the separately reviewed selection receipt. It rejects partial stock results, changed process/source/helper pins and any between-phase file difference except the two expected marker changes. Each phase rejects any selection change during its own inspection. Neither helper nor a passed receipt schedules the next phase.

The selection receipt must cite and hash its supporting evidence, not just assert review booleans: the same-process independent stock-review JSON, before/after UI images and their WindowBridge receipts, and bounded stock-inspection/selection log extracts with source path and byte offsets. Capture receipts must bind each image hash to the exact editor process and reviewed phase order. All supporting files are rehashed by the diagnostic guard. These checks prove attribution and integrity; the owner still interprets UI/log contents. See the preparation record for the exact proposed local receipt schema.

Before consuming a one-use dispatch claim, manifest review rejects null/malformed inventories, wrong roots, duplicate or noncanonical paths, invalid pins and exceeded bounds. Native admission then reads a fresh bounded whole-mod inventory and rejects any mismatch **before** creating the claim. The existing in-phase checks remain, so a later race still stops the admitted phase without automatic retry.

- Per phase: at most **2 root finds, 5 subobject finds and 5 exports**. Stock loads: **0**. Diagnostic loads: **at most 1**, only the exact missing root. No extra reference-resolution calls or alternate paths. All root/object identities use the fixed full path, class and immediate outer; outer traversal is capped at 8 and lexical nesting at 16.
- Native helper timing: pre-dispatch inventory admission has a **90-second cooperative budget**, followed by a separate **90-second cooperative inspection budget** after admission. These are not one shared deadline. Other limits: at least **2 GiB free disk**, **1 MiB per export/inventory**, **8 MiB aggregate raw exports** and **8 MiB aggregate inventories**, **128 KiB per receipt**. Synchronous engine work cannot be interrupted by these checks. No fixed RAM reserve is reinstated; actual allocation failures stop.
- Whole mod inventory: at most **256 files, 512 MiB and 1,024 directories across both roots**; reject redirects/reparse points. Check before and after each phase, with the original 27 protected pins and exact phase marker state checked between native operations. An incomplete final inventory is `UNKNOWN`, not unchanged.
- Supporting evidence: exactly two UI images (at most **8 MiB each**) with pinned receipts (at most **128 KiB each**), one independent stock review (at most **128 KiB**), and two log extracts (at most **256 KiB each**). Require ordered capture timestamps between the stock and diagnostic manifest review times, matching process identities, and ordered nonoverlapping byte spans from the same named source log. Missing evidence or a changed hash stops admission or the diagnostic guard.
- Owner-supervised selection and restoration each allow **one attempt**, at most **5 read-only state polls over 15 seconds**. Review a bounded **256 KiB log delta** for each transition and native phase; excess, missing/truncated/rotated log coverage is an unresolved stop. The inspector does not monitor warning text or own UI input: the owner must review the external log span before authorizing another phase. No blind repeated clicks or background continuation.
- Stop requests are honored by placing `STOP` in the attempt or current phase output directory, and by the owner ceasing editor input. A stopped native call may finish before the next cooperative check. Preserve the persistent dispatch claim after any outcome; do not reuse a consumed phase.

The [preparation record](OBSERVER_DIAGNOSTIC_TEMPLATE_SELECTION_PREPARATION_2026-09-26.md) documents focused tests, exact helper identity and outstanding native prerequisites. The manifest template remains dormant; it must not assert current process/ownership or a selection review based on these historical notes.

Record `TEMPLATE_SOURCE_COMPARISON_SUPPORTED` only for objects actually inspected on both sides with attributable exact identities and sufficient fields to compare. Otherwise record `TEMPLATE_HEALTH_UNKNOWN` with the specific mount, identity, field or reference gap. Even a complete source comparison does not establish cooked package behavior, retail controller selection or observer creation. The intermittent localization-validator ensure needs its own disposition before the [package preflight](OBSERVER_DIAGNOSTIC_CONTROL_PACKAGE_PREFLIGHT_2026-09-25.md) can advance.
