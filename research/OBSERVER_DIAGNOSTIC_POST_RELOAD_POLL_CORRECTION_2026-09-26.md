An additional independent read-only review of the [101-test offline implementation](OBSERVER_DIAGNOSTIC_POST_RELOAD_PREPARATION_2026-09-26.md) identified an admission gap before any native attempt. The transition validator accepted a failed, refused or timed-out observation if it contained a nonempty reason and claimed no unsafe conditions. Two later successful observations could then satisfy admission even when the earlier reason described lost focus or ownership.

The [evidence validator](../scripts/DiagnosticPostReloadEvidence.py) now conservatively requires **every recorded poll to have result OBSERVED**. FAILED, REFUSED and TIMED_OUT each stop admission regardless of reason or an empty unsafe-condition list. They still count toward the five-poll cap and must remain in the evidence. Later successful observations cannot repair the attempt. The same rule applies to selection and restoration; it adds no retry or automatic recovery. A later recovery needs its own reviewed unit.
