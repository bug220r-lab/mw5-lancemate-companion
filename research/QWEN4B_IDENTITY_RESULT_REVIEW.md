## Decision and limits

The explicit actor/profile/callsign fields reached every measured prompt correctly, but their presence did not make this model reliably respect speaker identity. The two counterfactual pairs did not establish the required own/teammate distinction; the ammunition reversal, contradictory-request answer and unbound smoke contain additional clear factual or identity failures. Preserve the source-supported projection improvement without presenting it as proven model behavior.

The [previous twelve-case review](QWEN4B_PROMPT_CACHE_RESULT_REVIEW.md) remains valid, including its six failures involving invented action receipts, mission results, identity, quoted instructions, missing uncertainty and route confirmation. Those different cases were not rerun under this serializer, so this narrower diagnostic neither resolves them nor proves the final prompt would produce the exact same earlier outputs. No controlled before/after improvement, general factual acceptance, personal-style acceptance or release readiness follows.

Keep this configuration out of live factual mission speech and tactical authority. The complete mod, conversational milestone, spoken-latency target and game-control milestones remain unachieved. Any further model experiment needs a separately specified scope and preserved prior evidence; this review does not launch or authorize another run.
